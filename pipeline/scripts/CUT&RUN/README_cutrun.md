It records alignment products, normalized signal tracks, peak calls, promoter-binding evidence, intermediate figure inputs, and final visualizations for MCM3, NONO, PSPC1, and matched IgG libraries.

> `<PROJECT_ROOT>` is a portable placeholder for the directory containing the repository clone. This workspace is populated by `scripts/CUT&RUN/04_align_and_normalize.py`, `05_call_peaks_and_define_binding.py`, and `06_render_figures.py`.

## Signal tracks: `03_bigwig/`

```text
03_bigwig/
├── <FACTOR>-<REPLICATE>/<FACTOR>-<REPLICATE>.bw  # normalized library tracks
├── mean_intermediates/<FACTOR>_mean.bw            # unscaled two-replicate means
├── IGV_representation/<FACTOR>_mean.bw             # display-scaled mean tracks
└── YeastNormalization.tsv                          # retained yeast pairs and scales
```

`<FACTOR>` is one of `MCM3`, `NONO`, `PSPC1`, or `IgG`; `<REPLICATE>` is `1` or `2`. The replicate tracks are analytical inputs, not merely browser artifacts: Step 05 queries them to evaluate the promoter-level factor-to-IgG ratio independently in both replicates. The mean tracks are used in Step 06 figures and in Step 07 regulatory-target metaprofiles.

## Evidence generated by the workspace

### Alignment and normalization — Step 04

Step 04 preserves independently aligned mouse and yeast BAMs, filters mouse alignments according to the configured read-selection policy, and calculates a yeast-spike-in scale factor for each library. It converts paired-fragment mouse coverage into BigWigs and derives factor means. Chromosomes with no BigWig intervals are treated as zero signal during mean-track calculation, avoiding a failure on an empty contig.

### Peaks and promoter binding — Step 05

Step 05 calls pooled factor peaks against pooled matched IgG, filters calls by the declared significance and enrichment thresholds, and links passing intervals to promoter windows. It records both the final promoter-bound gene lists and the replicate-level factor/IgG support tables used to establish reproducible binding evidence.

### CUT&RUN figures — Step 06

Step 06 uses the display-scaled mean tracks and `data/figure_inputs/` to generate publication-ready coverage and metaprofile graphics. The `visuals/` folder holds final PNG figures; source tables and deepTools matrices remain in `data/figure_inputs/` to support traceability and regeneration.

## Retention policy

Do not remove `02_bam/` or library-level `03_bigwig/` tracks before Step 05 is complete. Do not remove `03_bigwig/IGV_representation/` before Steps 06 and 07 are complete. For archival releases, retain `YeastNormalization.tsv`, final peak/promoter tables, the environment specification, and the corresponding figures.
